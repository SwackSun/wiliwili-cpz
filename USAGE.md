# wiliwili for CardputerZero

This community port of xfangfang's wiliwili v1.6.0 plays Bilibili videos and live streams. Connect to Wi-Fi, then open `~/app/wiliwili` in fff. Guests can browse videos. Scan the sign-in QR code with the Bilibili mobile app to use your account. If HTTPS requests fail after startup, check the device's date and network connection.

Use the arrow keys to navigate, Enter to select and Esc to go back. Press Fn+H for keyboard help and Fn+0 (F10) to quit. Fn+Q pauses playback, Fn+W and Fn+E change episodes, and Fn+J saves a screenshot under `~/Pictures/`. Fn+A mutes the sound and Fn+S/D adjust volume.

Automatic display mode follows the LCD and HDMI connection. Start with `wiliwili --display lcd` or `wiliwili --display hdmi` to select a display explicitly. Use `wiliwili --bv BV18Bn36BE6S` to open a video directly. HDMI playback supports sources up to 720p on this port. Upstream and dependency license notices are included alongside this guide.
