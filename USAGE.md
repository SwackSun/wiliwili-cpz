# wiliwili-cpz for CardputerZero

This community port of xfangfang's wiliwili v1.6.0 plays Bilibili videos and live streams. Connect to Wi-Fi, then open `~/app/wiliwili-cpz` in fff. Guests can browse videos. On the LCD, the sign-in QR code fills the center of the screen. Scan it with the Bilibili mobile app and confirm on your phone to return to your profile. Press any key to leave the code screen early. If HTTPS requests fail after startup, check the device's date and network connection.

The package and command are named `wiliwili-cpz`. Existing wiliwili preferences and sign-in data are read from their current directories.

On the built-in keyboard, wiliwili-cpz starts with Fn locked. LCD Search switches to letters automatically. Type a query and press Enter to search, or select a popular query on the left or a history entry on the right. Backspace deletes the last character. Leaving the input page restores Fn navigation. Elsewhere, tap Fn once to type letters and double-tap Fn to lock it again.

On an LCD list, press 5 while Fn is locked (Fn+5 in letter mode, or F5 on a keyboard) to refresh the focused list. Use the arrow keys to navigate, Enter to select and Esc to go back. Press Fn+H for keyboard help and Fn+0 (F10) to quit. Fn+Q pauses playback, Fn+W and Fn+E change episodes, and Fn+J saves a screenshot under `~/Pictures/`. Fn+A mutes the sound and Fn+S/D adjust volume.

Automatic display mode follows the LCD and HDMI connection. Start with `wiliwili-cpz --display lcd` or `wiliwili-cpz --display hdmi` to select a display explicitly. Use `wiliwili-cpz --bv BV18Bn36BE6S` to open a video directly. For on-demand video, HDMI defaults to 720p when signed in and uses a lower quality if you saved one. Guests default to 360p. The built-in LCD requests up to 360p. Available streams depend on the video and account. Upstream and dependency license notices are included alongside this guide.

Hold the Zero upright with either short edge at the top to switch the built-in LCD to portrait (170 × 320). Menus, playback and the built-in Fn arrows rotate together. The key nearest the top of the rotated arrow cluster moves up: F in normal landscape, C with the right edge up, Z with the left edge up, and X upside down. Hold a long edge at the top to return to landscape. Laying the device flat keeps its last orientation. HDMI always uses the normal landscape directions. External keyboard arrows and typed letters keep their usual meanings on both displays.

In landscape the built-in screen renders at its native 320 × 170 with two-column browsing and four home choices: Live, Recommended, Popular, and Search. Live and Popular open their feeds directly. Search opens a single video result list. The Activity sidebar button opens a full-width feed of videos from followed accounts. The profile shows History and Watch Later under the account summary. In Settings, select a category icon on the left to see its title and options on the right. Use Esc to return from LCD pages and dialogs. HDMI renders menus and text at 1280 × 720 and scales them to the monitor mode. During native full-screen playback the controls use a separate 640 × 360 overlay to conserve contiguous memory. Full-screen video uses a separate plane for supported streams up to 720p. When you change outputs during playback, the video follows the connected display. The interface switches layouts when you return to the home screen.
